PRODUCT 3: ACCOUNTING OPERATIONS & RECONCILIATION AGENT
An autonomous financial operations engine automating bookkeeping, reconciliations, invoice verification, and month-end close for accounting firms, CPAs, and high-volume enterprises.
Capabilities & 8 Operational Pillars:
A. Document Intelligence:
   - Reads invoices, receipts, bank statements, bills, and credit notes.
   - Extracts vendor, amount, currency, invoice number, date, tax rates, and payment terms.
   - Detects missing fields, unreadable scans, and duplicate invoices.
   - Links documents to correct client accounts and accounting periods; automatically requests missing documents from clients.
   - Automation Type: Optical character recognition (OCR) / document extraction + deterministic validation rules + AI-assisted interpretation.

B. Reconciliation Engine:
   - Matches bank transactions to ledger entries, incoming payments to customer invoices, and outgoing payments to vendor bills.
   - Supports exact, partial, grouped, and date-tolerant matches.
   - Detects duplicate transactions, missing postings, bank fees, and timing differences.
   - Generates an unmatched-transactions queue and reconciliation summary report.
   - Automation Type: Deterministic matching engine with AI triage for ambiguous edge cases.

C. Bookkeeping & Accounting Classification:
   - Suggests general-ledger (GL) accounts for transactions based on approved historical classifications.
   - Flags inconsistent treatment of recurring expenses; identifies potentially personal, unusual, or misclassified transactions.
   - Prepares journal-entry drafts with supporting source evidence.
   - Factual Guardrail: Tax treatment and accounting policy strictly follow approved firm rules, never an LLM's unsupported guess.

D. Accounts Payable (AP):
   - Captures and validates supplier bills.
   - Performs 3-way matching between purchase orders (POs), invoices, and goods-received records (GRNs) where available.
   - Flags duplicate invoices, pricing variances, and discrepancies; tracks due dates and approval status.
   - Prepares payment batches for authorized human sign-off.
   - Control Policy: Never initiates a payment merely because an invoice appears valid; enforces approval limits and multi-tier payment controls.

E. Accounts Receivable (AR) & Collections:
   - Matches incoming receipts to invoices and customer accounts; identifies overdue and partially paid balances.
   - Prepares customer statements, invoice-aging schedules, and scheduled payment reminders.
   - Drafts contextual follow-ups based on invoice age and client communication history; tracks promised payment dates and escalates disputes.
   - Automation Type: Invoice-aging rules + scheduled event workflows + AI-generated communications.

F. Month-End Close:
   - Creates a closing checklist for every client across the firm's portfolio; monitors reconciliation completion.
   - Identifies missing statements, bills, receipts, and approvals; audits suspense accounts and unreconciled balances.
   - Prepares accrual, prepayment, depreciation, and recurring journal drafts under approved policies.
   - Generates a close-readiness report and highlights blockers.
   - Outcome: A unified, consistent closing workflow across the firm's entire client portfolio.

G. Audit, Controls & Exception Management:
   - Flags transactions exceeding client-defined risk thresholds; detects unusual vendors, amounts, dates, and duplicate disbursements.
   - Identifies missing supporting evidence or sign-offs; records immutable audit trails of who reviewed, approved, changed, or posted each item.
   - Generates evidence-linked exception reports for the accountant.
   - Policy: AI flags suspicious patterns; it never declares fraud based on an anomaly alone.

H. Reporting & Client Service:
   - Prepares monthly P&L, balance-sheet, and cash-flow summaries; explains material variances against previous periods or budgets.
   - Tracks outstanding client documents and unresolved queries; drafts management summaries and client-ready reports.
   - Provides firm partners an overview of work completed, blocked, and awaiting sign-off.
   - Policy: Reports are strictly compiled from verified accounting records, with AI explaining variances rather than inventing figures.
Business ROI: Saves 80%+ of manual bookkeeping and reconciliation hours, eliminates manual data entry errors, shortens month-end close by 5-7 business days, and cuts finance back-office costs by 50%+.
